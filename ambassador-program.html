QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a marketing certificate? +

No. A marketing certification is not required to become a Stocker Ambassador. We are looking for people who can communicate clearly, find businesses and represent Stocker accurately.

Do I need to know how to code? +

No. You do not need programming or technical skills. Stocker is the product; your role is to introduce it to businesses that may need it.

How much can I earn? +

Commission depends on the number of eligible customers you successfully refer and their active subscription payments. The program currently provides a 10% recurring commission structure for eligible subscriptions.

Can I work online? +

Yes. You can introduce businesses through online outreach, social media, messaging, referrals and other legitimate channels. You can also meet businesses physically.

Can I work in my own state? +

Yes. Ambassadors can build relationships with businesses within their local market. Stocker is building a distributed sales network across different locations.

Is income guaranteed? +

No. Commission is performance-based. Your earnings depend on eligible businesses you refer and their qualifying subscription payments.

How are my referrals tracked? +

Your Ambassador dashboard provides your unique referral link and records businesses attributed to your referral.
